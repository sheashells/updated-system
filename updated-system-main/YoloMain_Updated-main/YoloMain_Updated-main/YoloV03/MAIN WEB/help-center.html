Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in or create an account?

Choose Sign Up on the portal sign-in page to create an account, or use Log In if you already registered. If sign-in continues to fail, contact support and include the email address on your account. Never send your password.

Are the properties shown on the site live listings?

Some portal listings may be demonstration content. Confirm availability, pricing, and property details with the property team before making a decision.

Where can I review my lease?

Sign in to the tenant portal and open My lease. The portal may show sample information when a live lease record has not been connected. Contact the property team for an official copy or clarification.

Can I pay rent through the portal?

Payment features shown in the demo are not connected to a live payment processor. Confirm payment instructions directly with the property team, and do not email card or bank account numbers.

How do I report a maintenance concern?

Open Maintenance in the tenant portal, enter the property and unit/area, choose a concern, and add notes that explain the problem. When the live Supabase setup is enabled, your request is sent to the property team and appears in the Admin Dashboard. If you see a submission error or have an urgent repair, contact the property team directly.

How can I check a maintenance request?

Open Maintenance in your tenant portal to review your request and its latest status. Requests are tied to your signed-in account; the property team can also provide an update using your request number.

How should I interpret an AI property valuation?

The valuation is an estimate based on available comparable sales and market information, not an appraisal or guaranteed sale price. Estimates may be unavailable when there are not enough verified matching sales.

Why might a valuation not be available?

The service needs sufficient verified comparable sales and a deployed valuation service. If no estimate appears, try again later or contact support. Do not treat a sample screen or example figure as a live estimate.
